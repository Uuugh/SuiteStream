Material Symbols Rounded by Google

Source
https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/10ecc14790612ca8fd164e3f4ffe9c16fc50d5b7/variablefont

SuiteStream modified the upstream Rounded variable font by subsetting it to
the app's glyphs with Flutter's font-subset tool. The glyph codepoints are
listed in lib/src/theme/suite_icons.dart. The upstream font is licensed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0. See LICENSE-MaterialSymbolsRounded.txt.
