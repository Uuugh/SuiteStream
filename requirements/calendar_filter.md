# Requirements: calendar_filter

| ID | status | statement | test |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLT-01 | backlog | The Calendar filter chooses which calendars and event states the calendar shows, and what is printed. | — |
| FLT-01.a | backlog | The Calendar filter is one filter icon with a dropdown (UIC-12). It lists every calendar with its color and the event states (Edited, Changed by the source since the edit, Hidden from Google, Deleted); choosing items shows only those events. It changes only what the calendar shows: nothing changes in Sources, Feeds, Rules or the other sections. | — |
| FLT-01.b | backlog | The Calendar filter follows the common filter rules (UIC-21). | — |
| FLT-01.c | backlog | The Calendar filter's choices are also kept when leaving Full page (UIC-21). | — |
| FLT-01.d | backlog | The filter applies in every view and mode (Month, Week, Day, List, Full page) and Print follows it: what the user chooses to see is what is printed. | — |
| FLT-01.e | backlog | A calendar's name and color in the filter follow its source: renaming it or changing its color shows at once (UIC-13). | — |
| FLT-01.f | backlog | Under the calendar sit Calendars (each with its (i)) and Key (rule colors and the event states of FLT-01.a), side by side with a thin vertical line between, in columns of at most three rows. A click on an item dims or hides its events on the calendar and in Print, in every mode. | — |
| FLT-01.g | backlog | View All, an eye icon on the Key title's line, is shown only when something is dimmed and clears every dimmed item at once. | — |
| FLT-01.h | backlog | A rule's key name is cut to the length in DEF-01, with its full name as the tooltip. | — |
| FLT-01.i | backlog | The calendar header shows the title and controls only. The filter lists calendars and event states only, with no Timed, All-day or Added color labels. | — |
| FLT-01.j | backlog | Calendars and Key share the width equally with the rule between them; in a window wider than the phone-width window (DEF-01) each keeps at least the width of its own list. | — |
| FLT-01.k | backlog | Hiding Hidden from Google hides the events that are hidden from Google and not deleted. | — |
| FLT-01.l | backlog | Hiding Changed by the source since the edit hides the events the source changed since an edit and that are not deleted. | — |
| FLT-01.m | backlog | Hiding Edited hides the edited events whose source has not changed since and that are not deleted. | — |
